How to place an order

No checkout or account needed. Choose your piece, then message us to order.

  1. BrowseBrowse the catalogue and select your favourite product.
  2. ChooseOpen the product page and choose your size and colour.
  3. Message usClick WhatsApp or Telegram to contact NEXTDROP.
  4. Send detailsSend your product reference and order details.
  5. We confirmWait for NEXTDROP to confirm the price, availability, delivery details and next steps.

Order details to send

Please include all of the following in your message:

  • Product reference
  • Product name
  • Size
  • Colour
  • Full name
  • Delivery address
Delivery: £5 per order.Delivery charges and your final order total are confirmed by message before your order is accepted. There is no online checkout, so nothing is charged on this website.
Orders are confirmed manuallyClicking WhatsApp or Telegram opens a chat with us. It does not itself confirm availability or reserve an item. Your order is only confirmed when NEXTDROP replies to confirm the price, availability, delivery details and next steps.

Delivery times

Delivery times vary by product. We will tell you the expected delivery time for your specific order when we confirm it.
